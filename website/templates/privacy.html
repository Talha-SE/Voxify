Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

1. Data Collection

SONUS collects the minimum technical data needed to operate and secure the service, including session and diagnostic metadata.

2. Audio and Transcription Data

Audio is processed to deliver live and batch transcription features. By default, this website service does not permanently store user audio or transcript content. Data handling follows strict access controls and is not sold to third parties.

3. Security Measures

Sensitive service keys are stored in server-side environment variables and not exposed in client-side scripts. Administrative access is protected by credential verification and session controls.

4. Data Retention

Operational logs are minimized and retained only for security and reliability needs. Product teams should configure retention according to legal obligations in their region.

5. Billing and License Data

Payments are handled by Gumroad. Purchase and subscription metadata are managed by Gumroad for licensing and billing workflows.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact your SONUS administrator.
