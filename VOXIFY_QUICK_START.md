# Voxify Quick Start

Welcome to Voxify.

Use the steps below to download, activate, and start transcribing in minutes.

## 1) Download Voxify

After checkout, download the version that matches your system:

- Windows
- macOS
- Linux

## 2) Open the app

Install Voxify if needed, then launch it on your computer.

## 3) Activate your license

Open **Settings** in Voxify, paste your license key, and click **Activate**.

Your license key is sent to your Gumroad receipt email.

## 4) Start using Voxify

Once activated, you can begin using the app normally for speech-to-text and transcription.

## If activation fails

- Check that the license key was copied correctly
- Make sure you are connected to the internet
- Try activating again after a few seconds

## Need help?

If you still have trouble, contact support with your Gumroad email and license key.
